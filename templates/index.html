How to read this

A few guardrails for policy comparisons

  • Prices are reported in USD per litre.
  • Tax percentages vary within countries over time in this dataset.
  • Association does not establish that tax or subsidy caused a price change.
  • Regional averages give each row equal weight; country populations are not used.
